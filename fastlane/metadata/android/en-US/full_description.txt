MetroVerse combines YouTube Music and open RSS podcasts in one Android app.

Features:

- Browse and play music from YouTube Music
- Discover public podcasts through Apple Podcasts
- Import and subscribe to public RSS and Atom feeds
- Shared music and podcast queue, player, notification, cache, and downloads
- Podcast resume positions, 10-second seek controls, playback speed, and sleep timer
- Background playback and Android Auto
- Lyrics, playlists, library synchronization, and listening history
- FOSS build without Google Cast dependencies
- GMS build with Google Cast support
- Material 3 Expressive interface
- User-confirmed, signature-verified updates from GitHub Releases

MetroVerse is an independent GPL-3.0 project based on Metrolist. Podium was studied as a GPL-3.0 reference for podcast integration. No Apple account is required for public podcast discovery or playback.
