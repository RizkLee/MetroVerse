# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Android listeners who want music and podcasts in one application, including people with limited development experience who need predictable navigation and clear setup documentation. The primary workflow is finding something to hear, starting playback quickly, and returning later without losing queue or episode progress.

## Product Purpose

MetroVerse is a learning-oriented unified personal audio client maintained by Rizklee. It builds on Metrolist's YouTube Music experience and integrates open RSS podcast discovery, subscriptions, episode playback, downloads, and resume behavior using ideas studied from Podium. Success means music and podcasts feel like native parts of one app, with one library, search surface, player, queue, notification, and responsive navigation model.

## Brand Personality

Focused, familiar, adaptable, and transparent about its learning-project status. MetroVerse keeps Metrolist's proven Material 3 interaction model while using its own name, package identity, repository, icon, documentation, release process, and attribution.

## Anti-references

Do not present MetroVerse as an official Metrolist or Podium release. Do not embed Podium as a second application, copy its Material 3 Expressive screens, introduce a separate podcast player, or create parallel navigation and settings systems. Avoid marketing claims, decorative cards, unexplained controls, and podcast-only styling that conflicts with the inherited Metrolist interaction model.

## Design Principles

1. One audio system: music and podcasts share playback, queue, caching, notifications, history, and responsive app chrome.
2. Source-aware behavior: retain YouTube-specific behavior for music while using standard RSS semantics for podcast feeds and direct media.
3. Familiar placement: Podcast is a top-level destination; podcast subscriptions and search also remain reachable from Library and Search.
4. Progressive complexity: core listening is immediate, while feed URLs, downloads, speed, and subscription management remain available without crowding the main path.
5. Preserve user data: database evolution, queue persistence, and episode resume behavior must survive upgrades and failures.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve Android font scaling, touch targets, semantic content descriptions, light/dark/pure-black themes, dynamic color contrast, reduced visual clutter, keyboard/IME actions, and responsive phone, tablet, and landscape layouts. New controls use text labels or familiar icons with accessible descriptions.
